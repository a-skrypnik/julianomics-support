Privacy Policy

JuliaNomics is designed to keep your personal records under your control. This policy explains what the app handles, where information goes, and the choices available to you.

In brief: JuliaNomics does not operate an advertising network, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not give the developer access to your private expense documents. Some optional features use Apple services or TheMealDB as described below.

1. Who is responsible

JuliaNomics is developed by Alex Skrypnik (“JuliaNomics,” “we,” or “us”). Questions about this policy can be submitted through the JuliaNomics support tracker. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public support request.

2. Information handled by JuliaNomics

Depending on the features you use, the app may handle:

  • Expense information, such as amounts, dates, categories, notes, and monthly records you enter.
  • Shopping and task information you create.
  • Recipe information, including recipe names, ingredients, instructions, links, and photographs you choose to save.
  • App preferences, such as display and synchronization settings.
  • Purchase status supplied by Apple so the app can determine which paid features are available.
  • Recipe searches sent to TheMealDB when you use online recipe discovery.

3. Local storage and iCloud Documents

JuliaNomics stores working documents in the app’s container on your device. When iCloud Documents synchronization is enabled, eligible documents are stored in your private Apple iCloud account so they can remain available across devices signed in to that account.

Apple operates iCloud under your Apple Account and Apple’s terms and privacy policy. The JuliaNomics developer does not receive access to the contents of your private iCloud Documents container. You can manage synchronization in JuliaNomics Settings and manage iCloud through Apple’s device settings.

4. Photos and camera

If you choose to attach a photo to a recipe or other supported record, JuliaNomics may ask for camera or photo-library access. Access occurs only after your action and system permission. Selected images are used for the feature you requested and are stored with the app’s documents. You can change system permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

5. Online recipe discovery

When you use online recipe search or discovery, JuliaNomics sends the recipe request to TheMealDB and receives recipe text and artwork in response. Network services necessarily receive information such as the request and IP address. TheMealDB states that it uses Cloudflare for usage analytics, error tracking, and service monitoring. Its handling is governed by the TheMealDB Privacy Policy.

JuliaNomics identifies TheMealDB as the source where appropriate. Avoid placing personal information in recipe search terms.

6. Apple services

  • StoreKit: Apple processes purchases and subscriptions. JuliaNomics receives entitlement and transaction-status information needed to provide purchased functionality; the developer does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Siri and App Intents: If you invoke a JuliaNomics action through Siri, Shortcuts, or another Apple system surface, Apple processes that interaction under Apple’s privacy terms and passes the information needed to perform the requested action.
  • iCloud: Apple provides synchronization and storage as described above.

7. On-device generated content

Where available, JuliaNomics may use Apple-provided on-device capabilities to generate lightweight content such as rotating jokes. JuliaNomics does not operate a developer server that receives those generation requests.

8. Tracking, advertising, and analytics

JuliaNomics does not include third-party advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. JuliaNomics does not operate its own analytics or profiling service. Third-party services used for a feature may process service traffic according to their own policies, including TheMealDB as described above.

9. Retention and deletion

Your local records remain until you delete them in the app, remove the relevant files, or delete the app and its data. Synced documents may remain in iCloud until you delete them from iCloud. Apple may retain purchase records as required for account, tax, fraud-prevention, and legal purposes.

For practical deletion instructions, see Data Choices and Deletion.

10. Sharing and disclosure

JuliaNomics sends information only as needed to provide features you choose, such as iCloud synchronization, Apple purchase validation, Siri/App Intents, or online recipe discovery. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or necessary to protect users, rights, and service security. The developer does not sell or rent your personal records.

11. Children

JuliaNomics is a general-audience personal organization app and is not directed specifically to children. The developer does not knowingly operate a service that collects children’s personal information.

12. Security

JuliaNomics relies on Apple platform protections, including app sandboxing, device data protection, secure system networking, and iCloud security. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure, so keep your device and Apple Account protected and up to date.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when JuliaNomics features or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in the app or its release information.

14. Contact

Developer: Alex Skrypnik

Support and privacy requests: open a JuliaNomics support request.

For your privacy, do not place expense records, receipts, payment details, addresses, health information, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
