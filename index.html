Contact and assistance

How can we help?

JuliaNomics is developed and supported by Alex Skrypnik. For a support request, bug report, privacy question, or feature suggestion, use the public support tracker.

Open a support request

Do not include expense records, receipts, addresses, payment information, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.

When reporting a problem

  • Describe what you expected and what happened.
  • Include the JuliaNomics version and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • Use screenshots only after removing personal information.

Quick answers

Common questions

Where is my JuliaNomics data stored?

JuliaNomics stores its working documents on your device. If you enable iCloud Documents synchronization, eligible documents are also stored in your private iCloud account.

Can the developer see my expenses?

No. JuliaNomics does not provide the developer with access to your local documents or private iCloud Documents. Please do not post personal records in public support requests.

How do I manage synchronization?

Open JuliaNomics Settings, then open iCloud Documents Synchronisation. You can review or change the synchronization setting there.

How do I manage a subscription?

Subscriptions and billing are managed by Apple. Open the App Store account settings on your device and choose Subscriptions.

How do I remove my data?

See the Data Choices and Deletion page for device and iCloud guidance.
