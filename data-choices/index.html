Data Choices and Deletion

JuliaNomics stores your personal records on your device and, when enabled, in your private iCloud Documents. The developer does not hold a separate JuliaNomics account containing those records.

Delete individual records

Use the deletion controls provided in the relevant JuliaNomics expense, shopping, task, or cookbook view. Confirm the result before deleting any backup or recovered copy.

Manage iCloud synchronization

  1. Open JuliaNomics.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose iCloud Documents Synchronisation.
  4. Review or disable synchronization.

Turning synchronization off does not necessarily delete documents already stored in iCloud. Use Apple’s iCloud storage-management tools to review and remove stored app documents.

Remove local app data

Before proceeding, export or back up anything you want to preserve. Deleting JuliaNomics from a device removes its local app container from that device. Offloading an app may retain documents, so choose deletion—not offloading—if your intention is to remove local data.

Photos permission

You can change JuliaNomics camera and photo-library permissions at any time in the system Settings app. Revoking permission does not automatically delete images already copied into JuliaNomics documents.

Subscriptions and purchase history

Manage JuliaNomics subscriptions from your Apple Account’s Subscriptions page. Apple controls billing records and purchase history; removing JuliaNomics does not cancel an active subscription.

Need help?

Open a support request

Do not post personal records or other sensitive information in a public support request.
